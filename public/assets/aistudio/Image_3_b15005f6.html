Our Heritage & Soul

The Alchemical Hearth of Kathmandu

Founded within the storied shadow of the Himalayas, Himalayan Hearth resurrects forgotten Newari, Thakali, and Sherpa culinary arts. We source pristine organic ingredients directly from the cold mountain soils of Mustang, Dolpo, and Jumla.

Every dish is infused with wild-foraged Jimbu, high-altitude Timur pepper, stone-pressed mustard oil, and indigenous heirloom grains cooked slowly in hand-beaten Newari brass vessels.
