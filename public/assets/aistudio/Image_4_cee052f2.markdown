## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the timeless dignity, sensory richness, and artisanal depth of Himalayan fine dining. Rooted in Kathmandu's culinary heritage, the visual tone balances ancient Newari metal craft and clay ovens with modern culinary minimalism. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Editorial Luxury: expansive negative space, quiet parchment backdrops, generous macro photography containers, hairline metallic accents, and refined typographic contrast.
